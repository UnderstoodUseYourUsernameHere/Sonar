Made in Godot 4

## Nyctophobia

What it is: 
* Nyctophobia is a concept ive been interested in, so for this week of Hackclub Terra, ive decided to experiment with the ideas, its sort of like scanner sombre and the like, but in 2d! Using a procedurally generated map drawn entirely in code!

## How to use

Itch demo link:

Or, you can do this:

    Open Godot 4
    Open this project folder
    Press Play (▶) at the top
	
## How to Play

Its very simple, really, you just open up either the web demo or the project itself in Godot, then get to playing! You open up the start screen and find yourself in the game ready to play. Controls are rather simple:
WASD for movement, Space for pings, R to retry that specific stage, or N to get to the next sector! 

## Gameplay loop

The gameplay loop is rather simple, you try not to die to the enemy, which gets more aggressive and difficult to evade with each passing sector, and collect access cards to get you to the door! (That is, one SINGLE access card, i mean)

## Features

Nyctophobia, or however you want to call this game, has the following features:

- Lidar-like scanning, similar to scanner sombre, the scan ring is broken by solid objects or the monster itself 
- The monster can also counter ping in later stages, which it uses to find you whenever it is nearby and attack.
- With each passing stage the monster is more likely to guard objective points
- Sound effects whenever you ping, the monster pings, or you walk, courtesy of Kenny.nl. 
- 5 stages (6 with the tutorial first stage) to finish, which comprises the campaign. 
- The game shows you the seed and the objective in the hud drawn at the top right and top left respectively, though the seed is quite useless.
